Marketing is not advertising. In childcare, it's the lived experience of trust — built before enrollment, during the tour, and long after a child starts. You are the emotional face of the center. Here is what belongs on your plate.

☀️Your rhythm

Short, relational habits — most take five minutes. Skim by day, week, and month.

☀️Daily

  • Greet families warmly by name.
  • Notice one child or family moment worth celebrating.
  • Capture one classroom or culture story for future sharing.
  • Support first impressions in the lobby and hallways.
  • Send one short personal video touchpoint to an inquiry, upcoming tour, or recently toured family.
  • Check for any family who may need reassurance, appreciation, or a small relational touchpoint.
  • Recognize one teacher publicly or privately.

📅Weekly

  • Review upcoming tours with office administration.
  • Prepare personalized notes for visiting families.
  • Post or submit classroom special moments, birthdays, work anniversaries, and event reminders.
  • Identify one happy family who may become a future ambassador.
  • Plan one retention touchpoint for enrolled families.
  • Gather teacher wins and child growth stories.

📊Monthly

  • Review family experience patterns.
  • Improve one part of the tour experience.
  • Organize one appreciation moment for families or staff.
  • Plan upcoming classroom celebrations and visibility opportunities.
  • Contribute content ideas to owner/marketing leadership.

🧭Tour conversion mastery

Jared leads the tour structure and the office handles scheduling — your job is to build emotional certainty before, during, and after.

Send a video when…

  • A new inquiry, after the office sends basic information.
  • An upcoming tour within the next few days.
  • The same day a tour is scheduled.
  • Immediately after the tour.
  • Re-engagement after a quiet period.

Speak to…

  • Looking forward to meeting the family.
  • Something you love doing for enrolled families.
  • The center's warmth and parent partnership.
  • Something specific about the child or family, if known.
  • Reassurance for anxious or uncertain parents.

Ready-to-use scripts

Inquiry videoHi {{Parent}}, I am {{Director}}, the program director at {{Center}}. I just wanted to send a quick hello and let you know how much we look forward to meeting families who are exploring care with us. One thing I love most is helping children and parents feel truly welcomed from the very beginning, because this decision matters so much. I hope we get the chance to meet you soon.
Pre-tour videoHi {{Parent}}, I wanted to personally say that I am looking forward to meeting you on your tour. One of my favorite parts of this work is helping families picture what daily life can feel like here, not just what it looks like on paper. We are excited to learn more about {{Child}} and help you feel comfortable and cared for.
Post-tour videoHi {{Parent}}, thank you again for visiting us. I enjoyed getting to know your family, and I keep thinking about {{detail}}. One of the things I love most about working with enrolled families is creating a center experience where parents feel supported, not just informed. I hope you left feeling that, and I am here if any other questions come up.

Before the tour

  • Review family details shared by office administration.
  • Prepare personalized talking points.
  • Send a quick video touchpoint.
  • Ensure classroom readiness and visual warmth.
  • Brief staff on the expected visiting family when needed.

During — you own

  • Join the tour for authentic connection rather than full tour leadership.
  • Build emotional connection.
  • Tell meaningful child-centered stories.
  • Help parents picture their child in the classroom.
  • Demonstrate culture, warmth, and classroom quality.
  • Reinforce trust with a personal touch and a later follow-up video.

After the tour

  • Same-day or next-day personalized text or video touchpoint.
  • Emotional reassurance.
  • Age-group specific follow-up from the relationship angle.
  • Internal feedback to Jared and the office on family concerns, rapport, and likely fit.

What Jared owns during the tour

  • Leads the tour flow from arrival to conclusion.
  • Owns the meeting-with-family portion of the visit.
  • Covers the office portion of the tour.
  • Explains logistics, enrollment flow, paperwork, and operational confidence.
  • Positions the center as organized, professional, and family-ready.
  • Transitions families naturally to you for relational and classroom connection.

How you know it's working

  • Tour warmth and readiness.
  • Parent emotional engagement during the visit.
  • Director follow-up sent within 24 hours.
  • Tour-to-enrollment influence.
  • Family comments about feeling welcomed, seen, or reassured.

✨The why behind it all

Shared philosophy

  • Every interaction is marketing.
  • Speed builds trust.
  • Warmth builds confidence.
  • Consistency builds reputation.
  • Memorable experiences create referrals.
  • Retention is marketing.
  • Community visibility shortens the trust gap.

How families move forward

  • Families enroll when they feel understood.
  • Families stay when they feel known.
  • Families refer when they feel grateful and proud.
  • Directors convert trust in person.
  • Admin teams convert interest into action.
  • Community outreach creates future inquiries before a family is even searching.

Your role, in full

  • Join tours to create authentic family connection during the visit.
  • Send quick BombBomb or personal video touchpoints to inquiring, upcoming-tour, and recently toured families.
  • Create emotional connection early and reinforce it during the tour.
  • Make the center feel warm, personal, calm, and trustworthy.
  • Highlight classroom experiences, family celebrations, birthdays, staff anniversaries, and upcoming events.
  • Strengthen retention through family delight and milestone recognition.
  • Surface stories, photos, and classroom moments for the broader marketing system.
  • Support referrals by creating experiences families naturally talk about.
